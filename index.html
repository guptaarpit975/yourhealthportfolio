Privacy and security

Health data deserves more than a password.

Privacy is designed into how records are stored, shared and explained — not added afterwards.

A private AI assistant

The assistant reads only the signed-in patient's own records. Names and contact details are replaced with placeholders before any text is sent to the AI model, report files never leave our servers, and questions and answers are not stored.

Encrypted, record by record

Names, contact details, prescriptions, allergies and vaccination details are encrypted with AES-256, each record under its own key managed in AWS KMS.

Sharing the patient controls

Patients share individual records with a verified Doctor ID, keep a full sharing history and can revoke access at any time.

A workspace for each role

Patients, doctors, clinics and support staff each see only what their role needs. Support tools have no access to prescriptions or reports.

Reports stay private

Lab reports are stored in private, encrypted storage and are opened only by their owner or a doctor they have shared them with.
